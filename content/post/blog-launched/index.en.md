## A New Beginning

Hi there, and welcome to my blog! On October 7, 2026, this little site officially went live.

No grand opening here — just a simple starting point with this first post.

## Why I Blog

- **To record**: to capture what I learn and the pitfalls I run into. The palest ink is better than the best memory.
- **To share**: I hope these words might help someone facing the same problems.
- **To grow**: consistent output is the best form of input. Writing forces me to think things through.

## What to Expect

I'm not setting strict boundaries for myself — technical notes, reading reflections, and random thoughts on life may all show up here.

The only rule is: **write things that are genuinely useful to me, and hopefully helpful to others too.**

## About This Blog

This blog is built with the static site generator [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and the Stack theme, hosted on GitHub Pages. It's fast to load and requires almost no maintenance.

> The best time to plant a tree was ten years ago. The second best time is now.

So let's start right here, with this very post. See you in the next one 👋
